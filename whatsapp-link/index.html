About WhatsApp Click-to-Chat Links

A WhatsApp click-to-chat link lets anyone start a conversation with you on WhatsApp in one tap — even if they haven't saved your number. When someone clicks the link, WhatsApp opens with the conversation ready to go. If you added a pre-filled message, it appears in the text box so the person can send it with a single tap.

Where to use your WhatsApp link

  • Your website — add a "Chat on WhatsApp" button so visitors can reach you instantly
  • Email signature — let email contacts reach you on WhatsApp with one click
  • Social media bio — Instagram, TikTok, and Twitter bios that allow links
  • Business cards — add as a QR code or short link
  • Marketing campaigns — include in ads to give customers a direct line to your team
  • Customer support — reduce friction for customers who prefer WhatsApp over email

How the link works

The link uses WhatsApp's official wa.me short URL format. When clicked on a phone with WhatsApp installed, it opens the app directly to a new conversation. On a desktop browser, it redirects to WhatsApp Web. The phone number and message are encoded in the URL — nothing is stored or tracked by this tool.

Tips for writing a good pre-filled message

Keep it short and natural. Something like "Hi, I'd like more information about your products" works well. Don't write the full message for them — the goal is just to give them a starting point so they don't have to think about what to say. A good pre-filled message can significantly increase the number of people who complete the conversation.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a WhatsApp Business account to use this?

No. These links work with regular personal WhatsApp accounts and WhatsApp Business accounts. The link just points to a phone number — WhatsApp handles the rest.

Will my phone number be visible when someone clicks the link?

Yes — when the person opens the link, they see your number as the recipient of the conversation before they send anything. There's no way to hide it in a standard WhatsApp link.

What if the person clicking the link doesn't have WhatsApp?

They'll be directed to WhatsApp's website and prompted to download the app. If they're on desktop, they'll be taken to WhatsApp Web and may need to sign in.

Can I use this link on a button on my website?

Yes — copy the generated link and add it as the href attribute of a button or anchor tag on your site. Many businesses style it with WhatsApp's green color (#25D366) to make it instantly recognizable.
