What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is the standard placeholder text used in design, printing, and publishing. When you're building a website, creating a brochure, or designing a layout, you often need realistic-looking text before the real content is written. Lorem Ipsum fills that gap perfectly — it looks like natural language but doesn't distract readers from evaluating the design itself.

The text has its roots in a work of Latin literature from 45 BC — a treatise by Cicero called "de Finibus Bonorum et Malorum" (On the Ends of Good and Evil). The scrambled Latin words have been used by typesetters since the 1500s and became standard in the digital age when desktop publishing took off in the 1980s.

Why use Lorem Ipsum instead of real text?

When you use recognizable text like "Insert content here" or a repeated phrase in a design mockup, people focus on the words rather than the layout. Lorem Ipsum avoids that — it has a realistic distribution of letters and natural-looking word lengths, so reviewers look at font choices, spacing, and visual hierarchy instead of reading the copy.

  • Website mockups — show clients how a page will look before writing is done
  • Print layouts — design brochures, flyers, and magazines with realistic text blocks
  • App development — populate UI components with believable text during testing
  • Font comparisons — see how a typeface performs with a full block of mixed characters

How many paragraphs do I need?

For a standard web page section, 1–2 paragraphs is usually enough for a mockup. For full-page layouts like long-form articles or landing pages, 3–5 paragraphs gives a realistic sense of how the content will flow. If you're testing pagination or scroll behavior, generate as many as you need.

Frequently asked questions

Is Lorem Ipsum actual Latin?

It's derived from Latin, but the words have been scrambled and rearranged — so it's not grammatically correct Latin. A reader who knows Latin would find it meaningless, which is exactly the point: it fills space without conveying any actual message.

Can I use Lorem Ipsum on a live website?

You can, but you shouldn't leave it there. Search engines may index the page, and Lorem Ipsum provides no value for your visitors or SEO. It's strictly a tool for design and development — replace it with real content before publishing.

Are there alternatives to Lorem Ipsum?

Yes — some teams use industry-specific dummy text, real content from earlier drafts, or tools that generate more readable placeholder copy. But Lorem Ipsum remains the most widely recognized and universally accepted option in design workflows.

Why does Lorem Ipsum always start with "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet"?

That's the traditional opening pulled from Cicero's original text. Many designers expect to see it and use it as a quick visual cue that the text is a placeholder. You can turn it off above if you prefer a random starting point.
