Create Social-Media Posts Using AI

Use AI to organize and adapt your real knowledge—not to manufacture experience, recycle generic phrases, or publish unsupported claims.

5 modules45 minutes to 4 daysPublishable final projectReviewed September 20, 2026

What you will be able to do

By the end of this path, you should be able to select one useful idea, brief an AI assistant clearly, remove generic language, preserve truthful claims, adapt the post for different platforms, and complete a pre-publication review.

The originality principle: AI can help shape a post, but the reason to read it should come from you—your observation, example, evidence, method, or point of view.

Module 1: Choose one useful idea for one audience

Begin with the reader, not the platform algorithm. Define who the post is for, the problem they recognize, and the single takeaway they should remember. Trying to address everyone usually produces generic content.

Too broad

“Write a viral post about AI for businesses.”

More useful

“Write for owners of local service businesses who spend too much time answering repetitive customer questions. Explain one low-risk way AI can help, using the example in my notes and no invented statistics.”

Module 2: Create the first draft

Give the AI your audience, purpose, source idea, evidence, tone, length, and intended response. Explicitly prohibit invented stories, quotations, results, customers, and statistics. Provide source notes so the draft has something real to work with.

A practical prompt might request a clear opening, one useful example, a short explanation, and one genuine question. Avoid asking for “viral,” “mind-blowing,” or “game-changing” language; those instructions often increase hype without adding value.

Module 3: Make the post sound human

Humanizing is not adding random slang. Replace vague claims with specific observations that you can verify. Remove clichés, empty transitions, and exaggerated certainty. Vary sentence length naturally and keep only statements you would defend in conversation.

Generic AI language

“In today’s fast-paced digital landscape, AI is revolutionizing the way businesses communicate.”

Specific, verifiable detail

“I timed three routine customer replies last week: 42 minutes. A saved prompt reduced the next batch to 12 minutes, but I still had to correct two details.”

The second version is valuable because it is concrete and honest about both the benefit and the limitation. Never publish a personal-sounding story that did not happen.

Module 4: Adapt without duplicating

Cross-platform adaptation means preserving the central idea and factual claims while changing presentation. A professional network may need context and a business implication; a visual platform may need a concise caption and clearer scene; a community page may invite practical discussion.

  • Change the opening for how people encounter content on that platform.
  • Adjust length, pacing, formatting, and vocabulary.
  • Use platform-appropriate calls to action.
  • Keep facts and the core takeaway consistent.

Do not simply paste the same paragraph everywhere. Adaptation should respect what the audience expects without changing the truth.

Module 5: Review before publishing

Use a six-part check: usefulness, originality, accuracy, readability, platform fit, and intended response.

  1. Would this help the intended reader even if they never click a link?
  2. What detail or perspective makes it genuinely yours?
  3. Can every factual claim be supported?
  4. Can the main point be understood quickly?
  5. Does the structure fit the chosen platform?
  6. Is the requested response natural and clear?

If a draft contains a statistic, quotation, customer example, or current fact, verify it before publishing. If you cannot support it, remove it or clearly label uncertainty.

Practice project

Choose one problem your intended audience regularly encounters. Write three source notes from your own knowledge, create a first draft with AI, replace every generic sentence with specific value, verify each claim, and adapt the finished idea for two platforms.
