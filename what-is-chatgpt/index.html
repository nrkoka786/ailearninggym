What Is ChatGPT? OpenAI's AI Assistant Explained

📅 Published July 2026 · Reviewed September 27, 2026  |  ⏱ 7 min read  |  🏢 Made by OpenAI

ChatGPT is an AI assistant made by OpenAI that lets you have a conversation with a large language model. Since its launch in November 2022, it has become the most widely used AI product in the world, with hundreds of millions of users. This guide explains what ChatGPT is, how it evolved, what it can do, and how it fits into the AI landscape today.

What is ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is a conversational interface built on OpenAI's family of AI models. When you type a message, a model generates a response using patterns learned during training. Depending on the request and available tools, ChatGPT may also search the web or work with files and images. A fluent answer can still be wrong, so verify important claims and sources.

The chat format made GPT-class AI accessible for the first time. Instead of needing programming skills to call an API, anyone could open a browser and start a conversation. That simplicity drove the fastest consumer product adoption in tech history — reaching 100 million users in just two months after launch.

Who makes ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is made by OpenAI, a company founded in 2015 in San Francisco. OpenAI started as a nonprofit research lab with backing from figures including Elon Musk, Sam Altman, Peter Thiel, and Reid Hoffman. It later became a "capped-profit" company to attract the investment needed for large-scale AI research. Sam Altman is the CEO. Microsoft has invested heavily in OpenAI and has integrated its models throughout the Microsoft product ecosystem, including GitHub Copilot, Bing, and Microsoft 365 Copilot.

How ChatGPT has evolved

OpenAI has released several generations of GPT models since 2018. The models and modes available inside ChatGPT change regularly, and access can vary by plan, region, usage limits, and product updates. For the current lineup, check the model picker in ChatGPT and OpenAI’s official pricing page rather than relying on an older model list.

What can ChatGPT do?

ChatGPT supports a wide range of tasks, including:

  • Writing and editing — drafting emails, essays, blog posts, marketing copy, resumes
  • Coding — writing, explaining, and debugging code in most programming languages
  • Image analysis — reading charts, photos, screenshots, and documents you upload
  • Image generation — creating images from text prompts using DALL-E (built into the Plus and Team plans)
  • Web search — finding current information and showing sources when search is available for the request
  • Voice conversation — talking back and forth with a surprisingly natural voice interface
  • Custom GPTs — building or using specialized chatbots tailored to specific tasks or datasets
  • Data analysis — uploading spreadsheets and asking questions or creating charts
Availability changes: Model choices, usage limits, context capacity, and tools can change. Check ChatGPT itself and OpenAI’s official documentation before making a purchase or planning a workflow around a specific limit.

ChatGPT plans and pricing

ChatGPT offers a free plan and several paid plans. Features and limits change over time, so review OpenAI’s current ChatGPT pricing page before choosing a plan. Beginners can start with the free plan and upgrade only if they regularly need higher limits or additional features.

How ChatGPT compares to Claude and Grok

All three assistants can help with writing, analysis, brainstorming, and coding, but their model choices, tools, limits, and privacy options evolve quickly. Compare them using the task you actually need to complete, then verify current features on each provider’s official site.

Frequently asked questions

Is ChatGPT free to use?

Yes. ChatGPT offers a free plan, while paid plans provide different features and usage limits. Check OpenAI's current pricing page for details.

Does ChatGPT search the internet?

ChatGPT can search the web for current information. Availability and limits may depend on the request and plan, so confirm important claims using the cited sources.

Can ChatGPT generate images?

ChatGPT can create images when image generation is available for your account and plan. Availability and limits may change.

Is ChatGPT safe to use for sensitive work?

Do not paste confidential or sensitive information into any AI service unless its current data controls and your organization's policies permit it. Review OpenAI's privacy and data-control settings because practices can differ by product and plan.

What's the difference between ChatGPT and the OpenAI API?

ChatGPT is the consumer product — a chat interface you use in a browser or app. The OpenAI API is a developer service that lets you build your own applications on top of GPT models. They use the same underlying AI, but the API gives developers more control and flexibility.
