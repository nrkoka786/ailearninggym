About AI Learning Gym

AI Learning Gym is being built to help people learn practical AI skills around a real outcome. A learner should be able to describe what they want to accomplish, follow a focused path, practice the skill, and check their understanding instead of searching through a large library of generic lessons.

The site began as a collection of free, privacy-friendly browser tools. Those tools remain available, but they now support a broader purpose: helping learners use AI thoughtfully for writing, communication, creative work, research, development, and everyday tasks.

How the learning experience is designed

Learning paths are organized around five active steps: Learn, Show Me, Try It, Test Me, and Ask AI. The personalized builder considers the learner's goal, starting knowledge, preferred depth, and available time before recommending a quick skill, focused skill, multi-week skill path, or deeper learning path.

Our public curriculum covers AI prompt writing fundamentals, ChatGPT foundations, AI-assisted email writing, and AI-assisted social-media writing. Each public path identifies its outcome, syllabus, practice project, authoring team, and review date.

Editorial responsibility

Learning material is prepared and reviewed by the AI Learning Gym Editorial Team. AI may assist the drafting process, but a human editor defines the outcome, reviews examples and limitations, tests the lesson flow, and approves publication. Read our Editorial Standards and Site and Curriculum Updates.

Every path should lead to something the learner can actually do. Examples and exercises are written for the stated learning outcome, and important AI outputs should be reviewed for accuracy, privacy, tone, and fit before they are used.

What we offer

The site combines practical learning resources with supporting tools:

  • AI learning — goal-based paths for using AI in everyday work, beginning with ChatGPT fundamentals, email writing, and social-media writing
  • AI practice tools — token counter, prompt builder, and image prompt generator used to apply concepts from the learning paths
  • Text tools — word counter, case converter, diff checker, markdown editor, lorem ipsum generator, text-to-speech, font generator, and AI content detector
  • Developer utilities — JSON formatter, Base64 encoder/decoder, password generator, QR code generator, image resizer, and WhatsApp link creator
  • Calculators — loan amortization, tip splitter, unit converter, age calculator, BMI, BMR/TDEE, and sleep cycle planner
  • Productivity tools — Pomodoro timer, online clock with world time zones, stopwatch, and countdown timer
  • Screen utilities — color picker and dead pixel tester

Privacy

The browser tools, path builder, exercises, scores, and progress process or store their inputs locally as explained on the relevant page. When you choose to copy an Ask AI coaching prompt into an external service, that service's privacy policy applies. AI Learning Gym does not automatically transmit your lesson answers to an AI model, and we don't sell personal data. Read the full details in our Privacy Policy.

Accuracy

We try to keep calculations accurate and formulas up to date. The loan calculator uses the standard amortization formula. The BMR calculator uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation. The sleep calculator is based on the 90-minute sleep cycle model. If you notice an error in any tool, please let us know via the contact page.
