Editorial standards

AI Learning Gym is an independently operated educational website focused on practical, beginner-friendly AI learning. AI tools may assist with drafting and organizing lesson material, but every published learning path is manually reviewed, edited, and tested before publication. Lessons are periodically updated as AI tools and best practices change.

Our goal is usefulness, not content volume. Each learning path should help a learner complete a specific real-world task more safely and effectively.

Who creates and reviews the lessons

Learning paths are prepared by the AI Learning Gym Editorial Team. AI may assist with outlining, drafting alternatives, or checking clarity. A human editor selects the learning objective, reviews examples and safety guidance, tests the lesson flow, and approves the final page before publication.

Our publishing process

  1. Define the outcome. Every path begins with a skill the learner should be able to demonstrate.
  2. Build an active sequence. Lessons include explanation, comparison, practice, a knowledge check, and guided follow-up.
  3. Check claims and risks. We review factual statements, privacy concerns, unsupported promises, and situations requiring authoritative professional sources.
  4. Test the experience. We test navigation, examples, exercises, scoring, and progress storage in a current browser.
  5. Record the review date. Public paths show when they were last reviewed and link to this policy.

AI-content policy

We do not publish unreviewed AI output as finished instruction. AI-generated suggestions must be edited for accuracy, specificity, originality, and fit. Examples must not pretend that AI has personal experience. Learners are reminded that fluent output is not proof of truth.

Corrections and updates

AI products change frequently. We review core paths when a material capability, safety practice, or user experience changes. Significant site and curriculum updates are recorded on the Updates page. If you find an error, use the contact page and identify the page and statement.

Scope and limitations

Our material is educational. It is not medical, legal, financial, or other professional advice. High-consequence decisions require current authoritative sources and qualified professional judgment. Product names belong to their respective owners, and inclusion does not imply endorsement.
