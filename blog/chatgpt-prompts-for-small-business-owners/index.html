30 ChatGPT Prompts for Small Business Owners: Marketing, Emails & More (2026)

As a small business owner, you're the marketing department, the copywriter, the customer service rep, and the strategist — usually before 9 AM. These ChatGPT prompts for small business owners hand you a tireless assistant for the writing half of that job. Every prompt is copy-paste ready: fill in the bracketed details about your business, and you'll get drafts you can polish in minutes instead of writing from scratch for hours.

Ground rule: AI drafts, you decide. Your voice, your facts, your final call — especially on prices, policies, and promises to customers.

ChatGPT Prompts for Small Business Owners: Marketing and Advertising

1. "Write 5 Facebook ad headlines for my [business type] targeting [audience]. Keep each under 40 characters."

2. "Create a one-month marketing plan for a small [business type] with a $500 budget. Prioritize free and low-cost tactics."

3. "Write a 150-word 'About Us' page for my website. Business: [describe]. Tone: friendly and trustworthy."

4. "Give me 10 blog post ideas that would attract local customers to my [business type]."

5. "Write a grand-opening announcement for my new [business type] for local Facebook groups."

6. "Draft a referral program email offering existing customers 15% off for every friend they bring in."

Social Media Content Prompts

7. "Write 5 Instagram captions for photos of [your product]. Friendly tone, each with 3 relevant hashtags."

8. "Create a week of social media posts (one per day) for my [business type]. Mix of tips, behind-the-scenes, and promotions."

9. "Write a short script for a 30-second TikTok introducing my [business type] to new customers."

10. "Give me 10 poll or question ideas to boost engagement on my business Facebook page."

11. "Turn this customer testimonial into 3 different social media posts: [paste testimonial]."

Email Prompts That Get Replies

12. "Write a welcome email for new customers of my [business type]. Warm, under 150 words, with a 10% discount code mention."

13. "Draft a polite follow-up email for a quote I sent 5 days ago with no reply."

14. "Write an abandoned-cart email for my online store. Friendly nudge, not pushy."

15. "Create a monthly newsletter outline for my [business type] with 4 sections."

16. "Write a professional email asking a supplier for better payment terms."

Product Description Prompts

17. "Write a 100-word product description for [product]. Highlight benefits, not just features. Tone: [casual/luxury/playful]."

18. "Rewrite this boring product description to sound exciting: [paste text]."

19. "Create 3 versions of a product title for [product]: one clear, one catchy, one SEO-friendly."

20. "Write a comparison paragraph showing why my [product] beats the typical competitor."

Customer Service Reply Prompts

21. "Draft a calm, professional reply to this angry customer review: [paste review]."

22. "Write a template for responding to 'do you offer refunds?' questions. Policy: [your policy]."

23. "Create an FAQ answer for 'how long does shipping take?' in a friendly tone."

24. "Write a thank-you message for 5-star reviews that encourages repeat business."

Planning and Strategy Prompts

25. "Help me write a one-page business plan outline for [business idea]."

26. "List 10 low-cost ways to get my first 100 customers for [business type]."

27. "Analyze the pros and cons of raising my prices by 10% for [business type]."

28. "Give me 5 upsell ideas for customers who buy [product/service]."

29. "Draft 10 interview questions for hiring my first part-time employee."

30. "Create a simple weekly checklist for running my [business type] without forgetting anything."

Tips: Making These Prompts Work for YOUR Business

The bracketed parts are where the magic happens. "My bakery in Austin" beats "my business" every time — local details, your actual products, and your real voice turn generic drafts into content that sounds like you. After the first draft, always do one revision pass: "shorter," "friendlier," "add our Saturday hours." And never publish a price, policy, or promise you haven't double-checked — AI doesn't know your margins.

One more technique worth learning: the example method. When you have a piece of writing you love — a competitor's product description, an email with great open rates — paste it in and write: "Write something in this style for my [product], but original." AI is excellent at absorbing tone and structure from an example. Just make sure the final content is genuinely yours in substance, not a rewrite of someone else's copyrighted text. Style inspiration is fine; copying is not.

ChatGPT Prompts for Small Business Owners: A Sample Week

How do these ChatGPT prompts for small business owners fit into an actual week? Here's a realistic rhythm for a one-person business:

Monday morning (30 min): content batching. Use prompt #8 to generate the week's social posts in one sitting. Edit them over coffee, schedule them, and don't think about social media again until next Monday.

Wednesday (20 min): customer communication. Prompt #16 territory — that supplier email you've been avoiding. Then prompt #21: finally reply to the two reviews (one glowing, one grumpy) sitting unanswered. The grumpy one especially: AI helps you stay professional when you're annoyed.

Friday (30 min): next week's pipeline. Prompt #12: draft the welcome email for the new customers who bought this week. Prompt #26: pick two low-cost customer ideas to test next week. End the week with marketing handled instead of perpetually "meaning to get to it."

Total: under 90 minutes a week for marketing, emails, and reviews that used to eat entire evenings. That's the real promise here — not magic, just the end of the blank page.

Quick Recap: The 80/20 of AI for Small Business

Eighty percent of the value in these ChatGPT prompts for small business owners comes from five of them: #8 (weekly social posts), #12 (welcome emails), #17 (product descriptions), #21 (review replies), and #26 (getting customers). Master those five and you've automated the most repetitive writing in your business. The golden rules: fill in your real details, keep your voice in the final edit, and never publish a price or policy you haven't verified. Start with one prompt today — the welcome email takes ten minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can ChatGPT really write my marketing?

It writes strong first drafts — headlines, captions, emails, descriptions. Your job shifts from writing to editing, which is far faster. The final voice should always be recognizably yours.

Will Google penalize AI-written website content?

Google's stance is consistent: it rewards helpful content regardless of how it's produced. Review, improve, and add your real expertise to every draft, and you're fine.

Is the free version enough for a small business?

Yes — all 30 prompts above work on ChatGPT's free tier. Pay only if you're using it so heavily you hit limits.

How do I keep my business data private?

Don't paste customer personal data, financials, or proprietary info into the free tier. Keep prompts about strategy and copy; keep sensitive numbers out.
