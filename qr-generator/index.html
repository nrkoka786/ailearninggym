About QR Codes

A QR code (short for Quick Response code) is a type of barcode that stores information in a grid of black and white squares. Smartphones can read them instantly using the camera app — no special app required on most modern phones. QR codes are one of the fastest ways to share a link, contact, or information in the physical world.

What can a QR code store?

  • Website URLs — link to any webpage, landing page, or online shop
  • Plain text — a message, address, or any information you want to share
  • Email addresses — open a pre-addressed email on scan
  • Phone numbers — let people call or save your number with one scan
  • SMS messages — pre-fill a text message for the user to send
  • Wi-Fi credentials — let guests join your network without reading out a password

Common uses for QR codes

  • Restaurant menus — link to a digital menu from a table card
  • Business cards — add a QR code that links to your website or LinkedIn profile
  • Marketing materials — print on flyers, posters, or packaging to drive traffic online
  • Events — use QR codes for ticketing, check-in, or sharing event details
  • Product packaging — link to tutorials, warranty registration, or product pages

How to make your QR code work better

Keep the URL short — long links create denser QR codes that are harder to scan. If you're linking to a long URL, consider using a URL shortener first. Print QR codes at a reasonable size — anything smaller than about 1 inch square can be difficult for cameras to read. Always test your QR code before printing large quantities.

Frequently asked questions

Do QR codes expire?

The QR code itself doesn't expire — it's just encoded data. However, if the link inside the QR code goes to a webpage that gets deleted or moved, the code will stop working. For permanent use, make sure the destination URL won't change.

Is there a character limit for QR codes?

QR codes can store up to about 4,000 characters, but the more data you include, the denser and harder-to-scan the code becomes. For best results, keep URLs and text short — under 150 characters ideally.

Can I use QR codes for commercial printing?

Yes. QR codes generated here are yours to use for any purpose, including commercial printing on packaging, signage, business cards, or marketing materials. The generated image is a standard PNG file.

How do I scan a QR code?

On most modern smartphones, just open your camera app and point it at the QR code — a notification will appear with the link. If that doesn't work, look for a QR scanner option in your camera settings, or download a free QR scanner app.
