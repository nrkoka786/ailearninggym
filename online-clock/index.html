About This Page

This online clock shows your current local time using your device's built-in clock. The world clock section displays the current time in major cities around the world so you can quickly check what time it is elsewhere for scheduling calls and meetings.

Frequently asked questions

Is this clock accurate?

Yes — the time displayed comes directly from your device's system clock, which syncs automatically with internet time servers on most computers and phones. It should be accurate to within a second or two.

Why does the world clock show a different date for some cities?

Some time zones cross midnight, meaning cities on the other side of the world may be on a different calendar date than your local time. For example, when it's 11 PM on Tuesday in New York, it's already Wednesday morning in Tokyo.

Does the countdown timer notify me when it ends?

The countdown shows an alert and highlights the display in red when it reaches zero. For audio alerts, make sure your device volume is on — the page plays a brief sound notification when the countdown ends.
